# Awesome Algorithms Education [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of courses, online judges, visualizations, books and competitions for learning and practicing algorithms.

Every entry is free to read or use unless marked as paid, and Korean-language resources are tagged so that both English and Korean learners can find what fits them. For implementations and contest-specific material, see [Awesome Competitive Programming](https://github.com/lnishan/awesome-competitive-programming#readme) and [Awesome Algorithms](https://github.com/tayllan/awesome-algorithms#readme).

If you want to contribute, please read the [contribution guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Courses
#### Introductory
- [Algorithms](https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/computer-science/algorithms) - Khan Academy / Free unit co-written with Thomas Cormen; articles and JavaScript challenges on sorting, recursion and graphs.
- [DSA Tutorial](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/dsa/dsa-tutorial-learn-data-structures-and-algorithms/) - Geeks for Geeks / Free roadmap of articles and practice problems from arrays and sorting to graphs, DP and segment trees.
- [Algorithm course for smart programming](https://www.inflearn.com/course/%EC%95%8C%EA%B3%A0%EB%A6%AC%EC%A6%98-%EA%B0%95%EC%A2%8C) - Korean / Inflearn / Free 55-lecture course by Prof. Oheum Kwon on recursion, sorting, trees, hashing, graphs and DP.

#### Intermediate
- [Algorithms and Data Structures](https://www.classcentral.com/subject/algorithms-and-data-structures) - Class Central / Searchable catalog of hundreds of free and paid online courses on the subject, with reviews.
- [Algorithms](https://www.edx.org/learn/algorithms/iitbombay-algorithms) - edX(IITBombay) / Six-week course on sorting, order statistics, string, geometric and graph algorithms; free to audit.
- [Intro to Algorithms](https://www.udacity.com/course/intro-to-algorithms--cs215) - Udacity / Free self-paced course by Michael Littman that teaches graph algorithms and growth rates through social-network analysis.
- [Introduction to Algorithms, 2020](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-006-introduction-to-algorithms-spring-2020/) - MIT OCW / Undergraduate 6.006 by Demaine, Ku and Solomon; lecture videos, notes, problem sets and exams with solutions.
- [Design and Analysis of Algorithms, 2015](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-046j-design-and-analysis-of-algorithms-spring-2015/) - MIT OCW / Undergraduate 6.046J on divide and conquer, DP, randomization and complexity; videos, notes and exams.
- [Introduction to Algorithms, 2011](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-006-introduction-to-algorithms-fall-2011/) - MIT OCW / Earlier run of 6.006 by Demaine and Devadas; lecture and recitation videos, notes, Python assignments and exams.
- [Classes and Teaching by Erik Demaine](https://erikdemaine.org/classes/) - MIT(Lecture notes) / Index of Erik Demaine's courses such as 6.006, 6.046 and 6.851, with lecture videos and notes.
- [Efficient Algorithms and Intractable Problems, 2001](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jrs/170/) - UC Berkeley(Lecture notes) / CS 170 by Demmel and Shewchuk; notes and homework on graphs, DP, network flow and NP-completeness.
- [Data Structures, 2014](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jrs/61b/) - UC Berkeley(Lecture notes) / CS 61B by Jonathan Shewchuk; Java-based notes, videos, labs and projects on core data structures.
- [Algorithms, Part I](https://www.coursera.org/learn/algorithms-part1) - Coursera(Princeton University) / Sedgewick and Wayne's free Java course on sorting, searching and elementary data structures.
- [Algorithms, Part II](https://www.coursera.org/learn/algorithms-part2) - Coursera(Princeton University) / Sedgewick and Wayne's free Java course on graph and string algorithms, compression and intractability.
- [USACO Guide](https://usaco.guide) - Free structured curriculum for competitive programming, from Bronze to Platinum.

#### Advanced
- [Data Structures and Algorithms](https://pll.harvard.edu/course/data-structures-and-algorithms) - Paid Content. / Harvard University / Extension School course by Michael Mitzenmacher on graph, approximation and randomized algorithms.
- [Algorithms Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/algorithms) - Paid Content. / Coursera(Stanford University) / Tim Roughgarden's four-course series on divide and conquer, graphs, greedy, DP and NP-completeness.

## Competitive programming
- [HackerRank](https://www.hackerrank.com/domains/algorithms) - Algorithm practice track with problems grouped by topic, from warmup to dynamic programming.
- [Codility](https://app.codility.com/programmers) - Free lessons with practice tasks on time complexity, prefix sums, sorting and more.
- [Topcoder](https://www.topcoder.com) - Long-running Single Round Matches (SRMs) with a problem archive and editorials.
- [Codewars](https://www.codewars.com) - Community-made kata for practicing in over 55 programming languages.
- [CodeChef](https://www.codechef.com) - Practice problems and regular rated contests.
- [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com) - English, Russian / Rated rounds about once a week plus Educational rounds, a large problem archive and community blogs.
- [AtCoder](https://atcoder.jp) - English, Japanese / Weekly Beginner Contests plus Regular, Grand and Heuristic contests, run by AtCoder Inc. in Japan.
- [UVa Online Judge](https://onlinejudge.org) - Classic problem archive with the uHunt progress tracker and the ICPC Live Archive.
- [ICPC Problem Archive](https://icpc.kattis.com) - Kattis / Problems from recent ICPC World Finals, grouped by difficulty and judged on Kattis.
- [CS Academy](https://csacademy.com) - English, Russian, Arabic / Timed rounds with dynamic scoring plus built-in tools such as a graph editor and geometry tool.
- [CSES Problem Set](https://cses.fi/problemset/) - Problem collection grouped by topic, from introductory problems to advanced techniques.
- [Coderbyte](https://coderbyte.com) - Coding challenges with video solutions; now primarily a candidate assessment platform.
- [HackerEarth](https://www.hackerearth.com) - Practice section for basic programming, data structures, algorithms and math, alongside hiring assessments.
- [Infoarena](https://www.infoarena.ro) - Romanian / Community judge with main, educational, monthly and ACM archives, contests and ratings.
- [Kattis](https://open.kattis.com) - Problem archive with thousands of problems and difficulty scores.
- [Learneroo Algorithms Tutorials](https://www.learneroo.com/subjects/8) - Tutorials that teach algorithms and data structures by solving challenges.
- [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problemset/) - English, Chinese / Interview-style problem bank tagged by topic and difficulty, with weekly and biweekly contests.
- [Rosalind](https://rosalind.info/problems/locations/) - Bioinformatics and programming problems, starting with a Python Village for beginners.
- [Snakify](https://snakify.org) - Python 3 course for beginners with over 100 exercises.
- [Sphere Online Judge (SPOJ)](https://www.spoj.com) - Large archive of classical problems with support for many programming languages.
- [beecrowd](https://judge.beecrowd.com) - English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese / Large problem archive with contests, formerly URI Online Judge.
- [PKU JudgeOnline](http://poj.org) - English, Chinese / Peking University judge with a large archive drawn from regional contests, plus monthly contests.
- [Aizu Online Judge](https://onlinejudge.u-aizu.ac.jp) - English, Japanese / Free judge run by the University of Aizu with over 2,000 problems from ICPC, JOI, PCK and introductory courses.
- [Project Euler](https://projecteuler.net) - Mathematical problems that need both insight and programming to solve.
- [Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com) - Annual December puzzle calendar with a new problem each day.
- [Project Euler @kr](https://euler.synap.co.kr) - Korean / Translation of the Project Euler problems.
- [Baekjoon](https://www.acmicpc.net) - Korean / The most widely used Korean online judge.
- [solved.ac](https://solved.ac) - Korean / Difficulty tiers and problem recommendations for Baekjoon.
- [Algospot](https://algospot.com) - Korean / Online judge and community run by the author of Algorithmic Problem Solving Strategies.
- [JUNGOL](https://jungol.co.kr) - Korean / Online judge for Korea Olympiad in Informatics preparation.
- [Koistudy](https://koistudy.net) - Korean / Online judge with problem sets sorted by algorithm and tier, plus contests.
- [CodeUp](https://codeup.kr) - Korean / Online judge aimed at programming beginners.
- [Codingdojang](https://codingdojang.com) - Korean / Problem-solving practice site for sharpening coding skills.
- [Programmers](https://school.programmers.co.kr/learn/challenges) - Korean / Coding test practice problems for job preparation.
- [Codeground](https://www.codeground.org) - English, Korean / Samsung-run platform with practice problems, user-created OpenRound contests and the SCPC collegiate contest.
- [SW Expert Academy](https://swexpertacademy.com) - Korean / Samsung's learning platform with problems and courses for software skills.

## Visualizations
- [Algorithm Visualizer](https://github.com/algorithm-visualizer/algorithm-visualizer) - Interactive platform that visualizes algorithms from code.
- [A Visual Guide to Graph Traversal Algorithms](https://workshape.github.io/visual-graph-algorithms/) - Interactive DFS and BFS explanations by Workshape.io.
- [VisuAlgo](https://visualgo.net) - English, Chinese, Indonesian / Animated visualizations of 24 data structure and algorithm modules with quizzes, by Steven Halim of NUS.
- [Algorithms Visualization](https://bost.ocks.org/mike/algorithms/) - Mike Bostock's essay on visualizing sampling, shuffling, sorting and maze generation.
- [Data Structure Visualizations](https://www.cs.usfca.edu/~galles/visualization/Algorithms.html) - Interactive animations by David Galles, University of San Francisco.
- [Sorting Algorithms Animations](https://www.toptal.com/developers/sorting-algorithms) - Side-by-side animations comparing sorting algorithms, hosted by Toptal.
- [Path Finding](https://qiao.github.io/PathFinding.js/visual/) - Interactive grid demo of A*, Dijkstra, BFS, Jump Point Search and other pathfinding algorithms.
- [Introduction to the A* Algorithm](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/a-star/introduction.html) - Red Blob Games / Interactive tutorial by Amit Patel that builds A* up from breadth-first search, Dijkstra and greedy best-first.
- [Python Tutor](https://pythontutor.com) - Step-by-step visual execution of Python, JavaScript, C, C++ and Java code.
- [Algorithm Wiki by Will Thimbleby](https://thimbleby.net/algorithms/) - Interactive wiki whose visualisations run from real JavaScript implementations.
- [TensorFlow Playground](http://playground.tensorflow.org) - Browser demo for tinkering with a small neural network.
- [Algovis](https://github.com/enjalot/algovis) - Curated list by enjalot of algorithm-visualization projects and tools such as Tailspin, Vamonos and Tangle.
- [Vamonos](http://rosulek.github.io/vamonos) - JavaScript Programming Language / Oregon State library for browser visualizations of array and graph algorithms with breakpoints and step-back.
- [The Sound of Sorting](https://panthema.net/2013/sound-of-sorting/) - C ++ Programming Language / Timo Bingmann's demo that draws and plays a tone for each comparison across dozens of sorting algorithms.

## Books
- [Elementary Algorithms](https://github.com/liuxinyu95/AlgoXY) - Free book of functional algorithms and data structures with 120 exercises, in English and Chinese.
- [Data Structures and Algorithms](https://people.mpi-inf.mpg.de/~mehlhorn/Toolbox.html) - The Basic Toolbox by Mehlhorn and Sanders, with chapter PDFs available for download.
- [Algorithms by Jeff Erickson](https://jeffe.cs.illinois.edu/teaching/algorithms/) - Free textbook and lecture notes from the University of Illinois.
- [Competitive Programmer's Handbook](https://cses.fi/book/book.pdf) - Free / Antti Laaksonen / About 300 pages introducing competitive programming, from sorting and DP to graphs, number theory and geometry.
- [Open Data Structures](https://opendatastructures.org) - Free / Pat Morin's open textbook on lists, hash tables, search trees, heaps and graphs in Java, C++ and pseudocode editions.
- [Hello Algo](https://www.hello-algo.com/en/) - Free / English, Chinese, Japanese and more / Beginner tutorial by krahets with animated illustrations and runnable code in 14 languages.
- [Cracking the Coding Interview](https://www.crackingthecodinginterview.com) - Gayle Laakmann McDowell's interview preparation book with 189 questions; solutions on [GitHub](https://github.com/careercup/CtCI-6th-Edition), author on [Quora](https://www.quora.com/profile/Gayle-Laakmann-McDowell).
- [Algorithm Design](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/Kleinberg-Algorithm-Design/P200000003259) - Textbook by Jon Kleinberg and Éva Tardos, published by Pearson.
- [Algorithms](https://algs4.cs.princeton.edu/home/) - Fourth edition by Robert Sedgewick and Kevin Wayne, with the book site's code and exercises.
- [Introduction to Algorithms](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262046305/introduction-to-algorithms/) - Fourth edition of the CLRS textbook by Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest and Stein.
- [Grokking Algorithms](https://www.manning.com/books/grokking-algorithms) - Illustrated, beginner-friendly guide by Aditya Bhargava, published by Manning.
- [Algorithmic Problem Solving Strategies](https://book.algospot.com) - Korean / Textbook by Jongman Koo that teaches algorithm design through contest problems.

- [Big-O Cheat Sheet](https://www.bigocheatsheet.com) - Time and space complexities of common data structure operations and sorting algorithms, by Eric Rowell.
- [stoimen's web log](http://www.stoimen.com/category/algorithms) - Blog series on classic algorithms and data structures.
- [What are the best and efficient ways to learn algorithms for beginners?](https://www.quora.com/What-are-the-best-and-efficient-ways-to-learn-algorithms-for-beginners) - Quora / Community answers recommending fundamentals first, textbook study and steady practice on online judges.
- [What are the best books on algorithms and data structures?](https://www.quora.com/What-are-the-best-books-on-algorithms-and-data-structures) - Quora / Community answers that mostly point to CLRS, with Sedgewick, Skiena and Manber as alternatives.
- [NEXTERS algorithm study notes](https://d2.naver.com/news/4850628) - Korean / Study notes from the NEXTERS club, published on NAVER D2.

## Competitions
- [ICPC](https://icpc.global) - The International Collegiate Programming Contest, the oldest and largest team programming competition for university students.
- [IOI](https://ioinformatics.org) - The International Olympiad in Informatics, the annual algorithmic competition for secondary school students.
- [Meta Hacker Cup](https://www.facebook.com/codingcompetitions/hacker-cup) - Meta's annual open programming competition.
- [AtCoder Contests](https://atcoder.jp/contests/) - Weekly rated contests for beginners to experts.
- [Codeforces Contests](https://codeforces.com/contests) - Frequent rated rounds, the most active contest platform.
- [Google Coding Competitions Archive](https://github.com/google/coding-competitions-archive) - Official archive of problems and solutions from Code Jam, Kick Start and Hash Code, which ended in 2023.
- [Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com) - Annual program that pairs students with open source organizations for a paid summer coding project.
- [KOI](https://koi.or.kr) - Korean / Korea Olympiad in Informatics / Annual contest for elementary to high school students with an online first round and a second round for its medalists.
- [UCPC](https://ucpc.me) - Korean / Union of Collegiate Programming Contest clubs / Annual three-person team contest with an online qualifier and an on-site final.
